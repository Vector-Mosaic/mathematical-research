# Provenance and the selected boundary

This case reconstructs a selected part of retained September 14, 2026 research.
It is the fourth example in the public collection. The selection followed a
review of actual Candidate, Evidence, Context and Strategy records; it was not
chosen from a hypothetical success story.

## The retained progression

An earlier Strategy retained unresolved source-specific signed-margin
questions. It explicitly distinguished weak limiting distributions or small
individual masses from the stronger control needed by the consumer.

The later retained Strategy described complementary mass as a provisional
restriction and revisited the Fejer and mass-CDF arguments. Partitioning the
physical clipped interval **before** pushforward gave the charge
`N sum_I M_I d_CI`, in place of `N M d_C`. Since each bin has small mass,
this recovers a factor of its width without throwing away any complementary
atom. A complementary interval split by a bin boundary contributes to both
pushforwards; its pieces still sum to the original atom.

The research combined that finite improvement with a stronger global
correlated-form motion estimate on fixed-order proxy blocks and a squared
Hilbert phase bound. The recorded
candidate then claimed an actual Jensen range with strict power `A < 2/19`
and a qualified logarithmic endpoint. Its consumer audit explicitly retained
the full affine budget and cross term, original scale, same source/predecessor,
profile, support, Gamma, residual and Rolle interfaces. Its standing remained
open and noncanonical. Recorded confidence and branch agreement are historical
interpretations, not proof certificates supplied by this public repository.

read-only owner interface at the same retained research cut. Returned content

fixture. Private transcripts, raw captures, service/account bindings and the
complete research store are not distributed. Consequently, an external reader
can reproduce the public computation and inspect its proof, but this package
does not independently authenticate the whole private research history.

## Public preparation

On September 23, 2026, coding agents under Justin's direction reconstructed the
finite argument, checked its interfaces, wrote the checker and authored the
store walkthrough. The execution is recorded in [validation.md](validation.md).

The historical finite margin `9/16` bounds the correction **beyond** the
baseline `A0`; the retained Hilbert statement explicitly says this. The public
note preserves that meaning and also gives a stronger sufficient condition
when a consumer wants a bound on the total. This is a distinction in the
chosen target, not a counterexample to the historical statement.

The public range calculation is conditional. It neither certifies nor refutes
the complete source-motion theorem or actual-Jensen composition. Open
dependencies in the walkthrough mark this package's boundary, not a claim
about the latest state of every private research branch. No independent human
review, proof-assistant verification, novelty or optimality is claimed.
