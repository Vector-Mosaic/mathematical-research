This example reconstructs a selected part of actual retained research. Its
public derivation and checker were prepared separately; they are not untouched
historical files or a replay of the original execution.

The failed historical script is not described as a passing computation. Its

## Later work and the boundary of this example

The retained snapshot also contains later conditional `R5` module and
whole-chain interpretations. They develop finite-parameter, residual, root and
recurrence arguments beyond the initial construction. This example does not
present the early report's missing step as the final state of all that research.
It checks the selected algebraic construction and states the analytic premises
needed to apply it to actual target coefficients.

The wider three-factor `R6` program likewise progressed. Earlier audits asked
for uniform parameter and higher-derivative estimates with fixed constants and
a common onset. A later retained context identified the remaining dependency as
a self-contained chain from the inverse and contour estimates to a common
zero-free moment neighborhood. Recording compatible modules had not supplied
that complete dependency chain. This is an unresolved proof dependency in the
retained snapshot, not a mathematical counterexample to the construction.

The public example does not audit or promote those larger `R5`/`R6` theorem
claims. It establishes the exact scoped results in [mathematics.md](mathematics.md)
and preserves the distinction between them and the remaining applications.
