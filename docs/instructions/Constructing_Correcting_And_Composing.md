# Constructing, correcting, and composing

This guide applies the common [RH instructions](AGENTS.md) to mathematical invention, correction, composition, and justified conclusions. Inquiry, creative exploration, legitimate substitution, warrant, verification, learning, and consequential dependence retain the meanings developed below. The public [architecture](../architecture.md) and [security](../security.md) explain the records, roles, and boundaries through which those decisions are preserved.

## Pursue the actual mathematical contribution

Choose work for what it can establish or discriminate in the Mission and current branch, not for the number of workers, records, tests, or elapsed steps it produces. Native reasoning, a scratch derivation, a counterexample, source reading, and a formal Session are possible means with different costs and warrants. Use formal execution when its exact frozen inputs, custody, effects, or reproducibility materially help; it is not the required form of every mathematical thought.

Explore alternative decompositions and representations before or after a favored route fails when they can reveal useful structure. A failed first attempt does not prove that the question is ill-posed; a fashionable route does not justify ignoring a better one. Match exploration to the question's importance, uncertainty, and expected information. Do not turn a narrow requested calculation into unbounded exploration or stop useful exploration merely because an initial template has been filled.

Keep speculative possibilities available as speculation. Strengthen them through an actual derivation, discriminating instance, or appropriately scoped computation. Distinguish constructing an example, proving existence, proving a universal statement, and exhibiting an effective or uniform procedure. An analogy can suggest a bridge without supplying it. Numerical support for finitely many instances does not establish an unbounded claim, and a counterexample refutes only a statement whose actual hypotheses it satisfies.

## Preserve proof obligations and useful reach

State the objects and exact result before deciding what supports it. Preserve quantifier order, domains, parameter dependence, constants, exceptional cases, limits, regularity, convergence, normalization, and the hypotheses required by a consumer. Use the strongest warranted conclusion: do not promote a heuristic into a theorem, but do not report an established lemma merely as a possibility because a larger target remains open. A valid result can be useful under narrower conditions than the Mission's final requirement.

Keep the proof-program boundary explicit. A theta/Stieltjes route or another selected program may demand an estimate or representation that a different program does not. Failure to satisfy that demand can rule out the exact attempted route without refuting RH or all equivalent-target approaches. Conversely, an asserted equivalence must be supplied at the scope and direction actually used; naming a familiar criterion does not prove the needed implication.

When a purported complete proof or disproof emerges, preserve the exact target assertion and available supporting references immediately for executive Candidate/A1 handling. Do not first rewrite it as a modest local observation, await consensus, or remove an inconvenient hypothesis to make it look complete. The constitutional standards and the independent review/Admission process apply to the actual claim, including equivalent-target routes. A novelty assessment is distinct from mathematical correctness; establish appropriate provenance before presenting a result as a new discovery.

## Compose by interfaces, not resemblance

A useful composition has a real producer result and a real consumer obligation. Recover both in full where the bridge depends on them. Align definitions, domain, quantifiers, normalization, assumptions, strength, and exceptional cases; derive any conversion or missing implication rather than assuming that matching labels make the pieces interchangeable. Preserve where independent premises or evidence are still required.

Use a substitute when it preserves what the actual consumer needs. This can be a legitimate local replacement without being globally equivalent, faster in every setting, or sufficient for every other use of the replaced ingredient. When a stronger ingredient becomes available, rederive downstream consequences. Do not inherit a now-obsolete limitation merely because it appeared in the old proof, and do not discard a consumer's independent premise because another part improved.

For example, removing a cutoff in one integral identity may strengthen a transform argument over its exact stated domain. It does not automatically establish a separate positivity assertion or a uniform bound needed later. Derive the new consequence, retain the proved strengthening, and name the remaining consumer obligation. If the improvement also supplies that obligation, follow the actual derivation to its stronger conclusion rather than keeping the old negative summary. This is a mathematical pattern, not an instruction to adopt a particular live proof program.

Cross-lineage retained material can provide a complementary ingredient or a warning about a concealed restriction. Use [grounding and discovery](Grounding_Discovery_And_Capabilities.md) when an unknown relationship or a named decision calls for it. A historical bridge proposal is a candidate for proof and distinct hostile checking, not proof by retrieval. A no-useful-delta result does not erase the original contribution's independent value.

## Check the uncertainty that matters

Choose a check for the actual reliance, consequences, and uncertainty. Resolve an uncertain foundation early when it determines which work is worth doing; otherwise finish a coherent argument before checking the result that will be relied on. Reuse unaffected valid evidence. A useful hostile check attacks an actual possible gap or reconstructs the critical implication. Do not add review rounds, computations, or evidence receipts merely to prove that prior checks occurred.

Preserve the distinction between independent derivation and common-cause repetition. Several workers can repeat the same mistaken premise, proof fragment, software bug, data selection, or source interpretation. Agreement increases warrant only to the degree its actual dependence permits. Conversely, a worker's access to shared definitions does not make every separately derived argument valueless; describe the genuine exposure and independence rather than using an all-or-nothing label.

Computational or certificate-based evidence must preserve its actual encoded statement, coverage, assumptions, arithmetic/error treatment where material, and checking basis. Successful execution proves only what that execution and its trusted checker establish. A tool error, timeout, inaccessible package, or failed Session is not a mathematical defect in the conjecture. Diagnose whether the failure is in reasoning, representation, code, environment, evidence access, or operation before widening its consequence.

## Correct the actual dependency

Identify the exact failed premise or inference, the conclusion it supported, and the downstream uses that depend on it. Preserve unaffected lemmas, useful residue, and valid custody. Correct or supersede the competent scientific owner and communicate the changed meaning to participants still relying on it. A caveat in a new message is insufficient if an active worker or Strategy continues to treat the old claim as established; an unrelated result needs no downgrade.

An Exact Refutation needs an exact witness satisfying the refuted statement's hypotheses or a valid proof of the relevant negation. Failure to construct a witness, repeated no-results, or failure of a method is not such a refutation. Preserve a scoped No-Result honestly, including reusable structure and what remains unexcluded. A materially changed question calls for a successor branch or the actual supported owner transition, not a silent rewrite of the old question's identity.

Learning includes recognizing when an old restriction, source summary, or mechanism is no longer useful. Revise the current useful understanding rather than growing an append-only timeline of every intermediate thought. Retain explanations, failed-route residues, and exact provenance when they can still change future decisions; remove superseded duplication only through the appropriate owner without erasing needed proof or custody. Researchers may identify an instruction or implementation defect and report it; that does not grant source-edit or operational authority.

Before consequential handoff, recognize the strongest combined target-relevant reach actually available, including historical ingredients made applicable by new work. This is ordinary scientific judgment. A materially uncertain complete reach, or a consequential negative judgment that may lose established reach, can warrant a bounded fresh-context review. Nothing here requires a review for every Candidate, negative result, Strategy change, or checkpoint. Preserve both the positive result and the exact unfinished obligation, and continue the work those facts genuinely support.
